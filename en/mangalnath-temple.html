मंगलनाथ मंदिर, उज्जैनA visitor's guide

Mangalnath Mandir stands on a rise above the Shipra river in Ujjain. The Skanda Purana honours Ujjain (Avantika) as the birthplace of Mangal, the planet Mars, and the Shivling here is worshipped as Mangalnath, Lord of Mangal.

Why people come here

Mangalnath is the main place in India for pujas related to Mangal: Mangal Bhat Puja, Mangal Dosh nivaran, Angarak Dosh shanti, and Kumbh and Ark Vivah for manglik brides and grooms. Families also do Navgraha Shanti and Kaal Sarp Dosh puja in Ujjain on the same trip.

Best days

Tuesday is Mangal's day and the busiest at the temple. Angarak Chaturthi (a Chaturthi falling on a Tuesday) and Bhaumvati Amavasya (an Amavasya falling on a Tuesday) are considered especially auspicious. Panditji can tell you the next such dates on WhatsApp.

What to wear

Traditional clothes are best. Men usually wear dhoti-kurta or kurta-pyjama, and women a saree or salwar suit. Red and orange are popular for Mangal pujas. Please avoid black.

How to reach

  • From Mahakaleshwar Temple: a short auto or cab ride.
  • From Ujjain Junction railway station: autos and cabs go directly to the temple.
  • By air: the nearest airport is Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport, Indore, about 1.5 hours by road.
