नवग्रह शांति पूजाNavgraha Shanti Puja in Ujjain

Puja and havan for all nine grahas, for balance across the kundli.

Message on WhatsApp Call Panditji

Navgraha Shanti is a puja for all nine grahas: Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budh, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu and Ketu. It is done when more than one graha is troubling the kundli, or as a general shanti before a new beginning.

Each graha is worshipped with its own mantra, colour, grain and samidha (sacred wood) in the havan.

Who this puja is for

  • More than one graha weak or afflicted in the kundli
  • Before a wedding, griha pravesh or new business
  • During a difficult dasha or Sade Sati
  • General peace and health for the family

Vidhi: how the puja is done

  1. Sankalp in your name and gotra
  2. Ganesh, Gauri and kalash puja
  3. Navgraha mandal and puja of each graha with its mantra
  4. Havan with the samidha of each graha
  5. Purnahuti, aarti and daan guidance

What to wear and bring

Clean traditional clothes: dhoti-kurta or kurta-pyjama for men, saree or salwar suit for women. Please avoid black. Panditji arranges the puja samagri. Bring your name, gotra and birth details for the sankalp.

Questions devotees ask

Is Navgraha Shanti the same as Mangal Dosh Puja?

No. Mangal Dosh Puja focuses on Mangal. Navgraha Shanti covers all nine grahas. Some families do both on the same day.

Can the whole family sit for the puja?

Yes. The sankalp can be taken for the whole family.

Duration
About 2 to 3 hours
Best time
On a muhurat fixed from your kundli, or before the event you are preparing for.
Where
Mangalnath Mandir, Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh
Booking
Directly with Panditji. No app or online advance payment.
