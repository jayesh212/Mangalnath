Questions families ask

Which puja do I need?

Send your date, time and place of birth on WhatsApp. Panditji will read your kundli and tell you which puja fits, or whether you need one at all.

Can I do a puja without a kundli?

Yes. Pujas like Mangal Bhat and Rudrabhishek can be done without one. For Mangal Dosh or Kaal Sarp, it is better that Panditji sees your kundli first.

How long does a puja take?

Most pujas take 1.5 to 3 hours and finish the same day.

How much is the dakshina?

It depends on the puja and its vidhi. Panditji explains everything on WhatsApp or a call, with no hidden costs.

Can my family sit with me?

Yes. Family members can sit together, and the sankalp can be taken in everyone's name.

Why is Bhat Puja done at Mangalnath?

The Skanda Purana honours Ujjain as the birthplace of Mangal. Mangal is a fiery graha, and offering cooling cooked rice (bhat) on the Shivling is done to calm it.

Visiting Mangalnath

Mangalnath Temple stands on the banks of the Shipra in Ujjain, a short auto or cab ride from Mahakaleshwar Temple. Autos run straight from Ujjain railway station, and the nearest airport is Indore.
